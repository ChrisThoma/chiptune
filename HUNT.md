## Everyone — not-a-bug list

Pseudo-locale artifact (round-3 #6 REJECTED; the same stray `lld` was noted under round-1 #13). Only clipping, wrapping and lost tap targets under that flag are findings.
- Letter suffixes appearing on an untouched track's label ("TRI" becomes "TRI B") when another track takes the same channel kind are a computed fallback for unnamed tracks, not a rename (doc comment on `Song.label(for:)`; round-2 #5 REJECTED intentional).
- Pattern names cap at 6 characters and track names at 16, each with a live counter (adaef8e, 7097d34). At 16 patterns the + chip disappears, and a trailing fade may cover the last selected chip; the leading fade also shows at scroll start. All accepted (c613c03; `.qa/hunt-2026-09-18/CHECKPOINT.md`).
