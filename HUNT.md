## Everyone — not-a-bug list
- Hardware Return writes the selected note at the cursor and advances; Escape only dismisses while a sheet is up. Both are mapped keys, not no-ops, and are deliberately left out of track-reorder mode only (ab3e413; round-3 #5 REJECTED).
- `-NSDoubleLocalizedStrings YES` doubles whole format strings, so one-argument formats leak `lld` and `@` fragments in the BPM/STEPS readouts, Export summary and ARR rows. Pseudo-locale artifact (round-3 #6 REJECTED; the same stray `lld` was noted under round-1 #13). Only clipping, wrapping and lost tap targets under that flag are findings.
- Letter suffixes appearing on an untouched track's label ("TRI" becomes "TRI B") when another track takes the same channel kind are a computed fallback for unnamed tracks, not a rename (doc comment on `Song.label(for:)`; round-2 #5 REJECTED intentional).
- Pattern names cap at 6 characters and track names at 16, each with a live counter (adaef8e, 7097d34). At 16 patterns the + chip disappears, and a trailing fade may cover the last selected chip; the leading fade also shows at scroll start. All accepted (c613c03; `.qa/hunt-2026-09-18/CHECKPOINT.md`).
- Playback stops when the app backgrounds and the transport reads Play on return (4dc7046, round-2 #1); route loss stops rather than switching to the speaker; the app forces dark appearance (APPMAP "Product Intent"). Editing notes during SONG playback pins the grid while instrument, tempo and arrangement edits deliberately do not; a muted track's header previews nothing and noise preview always uses one fixed hit (698bc11).
- Sections past 128 flattened play-throughs stay editable but neither play nor export; the orange banner is the mitigation and exactly 128 does not warn (36df550, round-1 #9). A lone arrangement section cannot be deleted (b866ae3). The empty library state is unreachable; do not force it (APPMAP). Export WAV is disabled with a footer when the arrangement reaches no notes (2a801f1, 31c4b2d) but stays enabled with a warning footer when every noted track is muted (758e025).
- Same-kind edits within 1 s coalesce into one undo step by design (0382e5f). Song deletion is permanent ("This can't be undone."); pattern and track deletion and both clears are undoable and say so (0532dd1).
- Track headers, step numbers and the REST key stop growing at Dynamic Type accessibility1 while the sound sheet keeps scaling: a deliberate clamp (4092007, round-3 #8). On iOS 17 the step labels still scroll sideways with the grid; pinning is iOS 18+ (bb82435 fallback comment in GridView.swift).
- Word-sized lock-free `ChipCore` parameter races are intentional and judged by the TSan CI job (ci.yml, 2b77d2c, fe5a0e3); only compound assignments on fields the render thread reads, or races outside ChipCore, are defects (dd2a3c5).
- Harness traps: the accessibility-tree walker reports the SongListView toolbar as an empty AXGroup though the buttons work (round-1 #5 BLOCKED after 2 fix attempts); the in-app build-stamp footer can name the pre-fix commit and the SIM-B Songs folder holds one stray UUID directory (both APPMAP "Tooling Caveats"); `idb_companion` crashed twice describing a scrolled InstrumentEditor sheet (`.qa/hunt-2026-09-18-2/CHECKPOINT.md`) — restart it, do not file it.
