## Everyone — not-a-bug list
- Track header/label suffix letters ("TRI" → "TRI B") appearing on an untouched track after another track switches channel kind: computed fallback in `Song.label(for:)` for nil names, not a rename (ISSUES.md #5, REJECTED intentional). Same auto-lettering is intentional for pattern names (APPMAP J4).
- Pattern names cap at 6 chars and track names at 16, each with a live "X of N used" counter (commits adaef8e, 7097d34). The cap is the design; only a missing counter on some *other* capped field is a finding.
- Playback stops when the app backgrounds and the transport must read Play on return (4dc7046; APPMAP: no background-audio mode). Headphone removal or route loss stops playback instead of switching to speaker (APPMAP intent).
- Arrangement sections past 128 flattened play-throughs stay editable but do not play or export; the orange banner is the mitigation, and exactly 128 does not warn (36df550, strict `>`). Empty arrangement and empty SongListView are unreachable by design (final-section guard, launch/delete always creates a song) — do not force them.
- Same-kind undo checkpoints coalesce within 1.0 s by design (0382e5f); cross-section repeats/patternLength coalescing did not reproduce 0/2 (ISSUES.md #3). Discrete BPM-stepper taps not coalescing is a lead, not a bug (APPMAP J10). Song deletion is permanent; pattern/track deletion is undoable.
- The app forces dark appearance (APPMAP); light-mode sampling shows nothing. Leading fade on the pattern chip strip at scroll start is an accepted cosmetic trade-off (c613c03). Export repeat stepper staying enabled at bounds is a known S4, not filed (APPMAP J9).
- Editing notes during SONG playback pins the grid; instrument, tempo and arrangement edits deliberately do not pin. Muted-track header preview is a silent no-op; noise preview always uses a fixed hit (698bc11). Tap on a filled cell is destructive with no confirm — acknowledged product gap, not a new S2.
- Word-sized lock-free `ChipCore` parameter races are intentional and covered by the TSan CI contract (2b77d2c, fe5a0e3); only compound assignments (`+=`, `&+=`) on fields the render thread reads are new defects (dd2a3c5).
